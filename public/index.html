Our Mission

Why ServeTogether?

Every day, millions of meals worth of food gets wasted while people around us go hungry. ServeTogether bridges that gap — one listing at a time.

🌱

Zero Food Waste

We help individuals, restaurants, and events share surplus food before it hits the bin. Every meal saved is a step towards a sustainable future.

🤝

Community First

ServeTogther connects neighbors directly — no middlemen, no fees. Just genuine people helping each other with a simple listing and a phone call.

⚡

Real-Time Sharing

Our live feed shows donations the moment they happen. Claim fresh food fast before it runs out — no sign-in required to see donor contact details.

🔒

Safe & Transparent

Donors share their contact info openly. You call, you coordinate, you collect. Simple, safe, and fully transparent — no data sold, ever.

📱

Easy to Use

List your food in under 2 minutes. Upload a photo, add your number, set your location — and watch it reach people in your neighbourhood instantly.

🏆

Impact that Counts

Every donation gets tracked. See how many meals you've shared and inspire others around you to do the same. Small acts, massive impact.
